Travel & accommodation

Before you book

Can I transfer my ticket?
Yes, free, up to 24 hours before. Standard and Workshop tickets can be transferred to anyone; Community tickets only to someone who also qualifies.
Are talks recorded?
All main-track talks are recorded and go to Standard and Workshop ticket holders within three weeks, then public after six months. Open spaces are never recorded.
Is there financial support?
We hold 80 free tickets plus travel and accommodation each year. Applications open in June and are decided by a panel that does not see names or employers.
What's the refund policy?
Full refund up to 30 days before, 50% up to 7 days before. If you're unwell on the day, tell us and we'll refund you — no note required.
