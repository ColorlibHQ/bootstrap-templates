Senior Frontend Engineer

Northwind Trading · Leeds · Hybrid · Permanent

The role

You would be the fourth engineer on a team that owns the customer-facing side of a logistics platform used by about 4,000 people a day. Most of the work is in TypeScript and React, with a design system we maintain ourselves rather than pulling off the shelf.

What you would actually do

  • Own features end to end, from the design conversation to the deploy
  • Maintain and extend the design system — roughly a day a week
  • Pair with the two mid-level engineers, deliberately and often
  • Take part in the on-call rota, one week in six, rarely called

What we are looking for

Several years building production front-ends, comfort with TypeScript, and an opinion about accessibility that you can defend. We do not require a degree and we do not run a take-home test.

Process

A 30-minute call, then a two-hour paired session on our actual codebase (paid at day rate), then a conversation with the team you would join. Three stages, two weeks, and we tell you either way.

£65,000 — £78,000

Plus 8% pension and a £1,500 annual learning budget.
