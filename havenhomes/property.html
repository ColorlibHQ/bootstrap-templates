Mill House

Mill House, Hebden Bridge HX7 8AF

£465,000

4 bedrooms 2 bathrooms 1,840 sq ft FreeholdEPC CCouncil tax E

A stone-built former mill manager’s house on the edge of Hebden Bridge, set back from the road behind a walled garden. Four bedrooms over three floors, with the top floor currently arranged as one large room and easily divided again.

The kitchen was rebuilt in 2023 and opens onto a south-facing terrace. Original sash windows throughout, all refurbished rather than replaced. Gas central heating, and the roof was stripped and relaid in 2019.

Hebden Bridge station is a nine-minute walk, with direct trains to Manchester and Leeds in around half an hour.
