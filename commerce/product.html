The Ridge is spun from a single copper sheet in a workshop outside Sheffield that has been doing it since 1946. Copper moves heat about twenty times faster than stainless steel, which is why it whistles sooner and why the base never scorches.

The lining is tin, applied by hand and re-applied free of charge whenever it wears through. The handle is cast iron, finished in beeswax, and bolted rather than welded — so if you ever manage to break it, you can replace just the handle.
