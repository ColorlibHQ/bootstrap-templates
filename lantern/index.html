The house

Thirty-four rooms, one very long lunch

Lantern was a lemon farmer's house before it was a hotel, and it still behaves like one. Breakfast runs until eleven because nobody has the heart to clear it. The terrace faces west, which is the only defensible direction for a terrace to face.

We are ten minutes above the water by the old stair, and forty minutes from Amalfi by boat when the sea allows it.

Dining

The menu is short because the boat was

Chef Lucia writes the card at nine each morning, after the harbour. Four antipasti, three primi, two secondi, and whatever the lemon trees are doing that week. Dinner is served from seven.

Before you book

Questions we get

Is the hotel suitable if stairs are difficult?
Partly. The main house has a lift serving every floor, and eight ground-floor rooms open directly onto the garden. The stair to the beach has 187 steps and no alternative — tell us when you book and we'll arrange the boat instead.
What is the cancellation policy?
Free cancellation up to seven days before arrival on direct bookings. Inside seven days we charge the first night. Non-refundable rates are marked as such and are about 12% cheaper.
Are children welcome?
Yes, at any age. Cots are free, extra beds are €35 a night, and the kitchen will cook off-menu for small people without being asked twice. The pool has no shallow end, so under-8s need an adult in the water.
When is the hotel closed?
From the second week of January until the first of March, while the terrace gets rebuilt each year and the family goes somewhere colder.
