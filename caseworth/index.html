Is the first conversation privileged?
Legal advice privilege attaches once a solicitor–client relationship exists. The initial call is confidential in any event, and we run a conflict check before discussing anything substantive.
Do you act on a no-win-no-fee basis?
For some employment and commercial disputes, yes — damages-based or conditional fee agreements, assessed case by case. We will tell you at the outset whether yours is suitable.
Can you act for both the company and its directors?
Sometimes, and only where interests genuinely align and both parties consent in writing. Where they diverge — which is common in a sale — one of you will need separate advice, and we will say so early.
