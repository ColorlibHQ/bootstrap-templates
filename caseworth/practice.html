Practice 03

Employment

We act for employers and for individuals, though never both in the same dispute. Most matters resolve without a tribunal, and the ones that do not are usually the ones nobody documented at the time.

For employers

Day-to-day advisory on contracts, policies, absence, performance and restructuring, plus the harder end — TUPE, collective consultation, senior exits and tribunal defence. We work on a retainer for around forty businesses and ad hoc for everyone else.

  • Contracts of employment and consultancy agreements
  • Disciplinary and grievance process, including investigations we run ourselves
  • Redundancy and reorganisation, individual and collective
  • TUPE on business and service-provision transfers
  • Settlement agreements and negotiated exits

For individuals

Usually settlement agreements, unfair or constructive dismissal, discrimination and whistleblowing. We will tell you at the first call whether a claim is worth bringing, which is not always the answer people are hoping for.

How we charge

Advisory work is either on a fixed monthly retainer or hourly. Settlement agreements for employees are fixed at £550, and in most cases the employer pays that as part of the deal. Tribunal work is staged — we quote each stage before it starts, so you can stop.

Common questions

How long do I have to bring a claim?
Three months less one day from the act complained of, for most claims — and you must start ACAS early conciliation inside that window, which pauses the clock. The deadline is strict and tribunals rarely extend it. If you are near it, call rather than email.
Will my employer find out I have taken advice?
Not from us. Nothing goes to them unless you instruct us to send it. If you are still employed we will also talk through the practical risks before anything is put in writing.
Do you offer no-win-no-fee?
For some discrimination and whistleblowing claims where the value justifies it. We assess suitability at the initial review stage and put the terms in writing before you commit.
