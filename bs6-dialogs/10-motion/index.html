Entry animations

Three entrances, plus the escape hatch.

.dialog-instant and .drawer-instant skip the transition entirely. That is the right choice when a dialog opens in response to something already in motion — a drag, a swipe, a second dialog — where an entry animation reads as lag rather than polish.

You do not need to disable these for reduced-motion users; Bootstrap 6 already honours the media query.
