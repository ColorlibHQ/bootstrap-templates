Mokka Bar

4.8 (312 reviews)·Coffee·Patershol·€€
specialtydog friendlycash onlyoutdoor seating

Three seats, one grinder and a roaster in the back that has been running since 2011. Kasper roasts on Mondays, which is the day to come if you want to talk about it, and the day to avoid if you want somewhere quiet.

The filter menu changes weekly and is written on a card by the till. There is no oat milk debate here — they have it, they will not comment on it.

Good to know

  • Cash only, there is an ATM two doors down
  • No laptops after 11:00 on weekends
  • Bags of the current roast are €14
