Patient information

Questions we get every week

Do I need to be registered to book?
Registered patients can book any slot online. If you're not registered you can still book a private consultation — registration takes about ten minutes at reception.
How quickly will I be seen?
Our median wait for a routine GP appointment is 2.1 days. Urgent same-day slots are released at 8:00am each morning.
Can I get test results online?
Yes, through the patient portal. Results appear as soon as a clinician has reviewed them, with a plain-English note explaining what they mean.
Do you accept my insurance?
We're recognised by Bupa, AXA Health, Vitality, Aviva and WPA. Self-pay prices are published on each service page — no surprises at the desk.
