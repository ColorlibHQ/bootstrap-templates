Fieldstone

A forty-year-old surveying practice that had quietly become a data business and was still selling itself as a surveying practice.

The problem

Fieldstone's revenue had shifted: two thirds now came from a subscription platform their surveyors had built for themselves and then started selling. Nothing about the brand said so. They were losing pitches to software companies a fifth their size.

What we did

Discovery ran three weeks and included sitting in on four sales calls, which is where the actual problem surfaced — the team could not describe the platform without first describing the surveying, and by then the call was half gone.

  • Repositioned around the platform, with surveying as the proof rather than the product
  • New identity system built for screen first, with a print set that follows from it
  • Website rebuilt around the platform story with a real product tour
  • Rollout across sales collateral, vehicle livery and two offices
