About

Design that survives the handover

I started out building websites for a print shop in Utrecht and never quite stopped caring about production detail. Most of my work now sits at the seam between design and front-end — design systems, component libraries, and the unglamorous work of making sure what shipped matches what was drawn.

I care about interfaces that stay usable at 200% zoom, on a five-year-old Android, and for the person using them for the eleventh hour that day.
