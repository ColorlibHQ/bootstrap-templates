What it’s like

Sixth Form at Northgate is deliberately less like school and more like the year after it. Students keep their own timetables, work in the study centre rather than form rooms, and are trusted to manage free periods without supervision.

Most take three A-levels plus an EPQ, or the full IB Diploma. We do not push anyone toward four A-levels — in fifteen years it has almost never improved an offer.

Subject offer

Twenty-eight A-level subjects run each year, including Further Maths, Politics, Computer Science and three separate sciences. The IB Diploma runs alongside with six subject groups and the full core.

  • Sciences — Biology, Chemistry, Physics, Psychology
  • Humanities — History, Geography, Politics, Philosophy, Economics
  • Languages — French, German, Spanish, Latin, Mandarin
  • Arts — Fine Art, Music, Drama, Design & Technology

Beyond the timetable

Every student takes on one leadership commitment: coaching a junior team, running a society, mentoring in Prep, or joining the student council. It is not optional, and it is the part most of them mention at interview.

The step up is real, and we would rather they take it here, with us watching, than in their first term at university.

Entry requirements

  • Six GCSEs at grade 6 or above
  • Grade 7 in each chosen A-level subject
  • Interview with the Head of Sixth Form
