Admissions

Four steps, no mystery

We publish everything: the timeline, the assessment format and what we're actually looking for.

  1. Register interest
  2. Visit the school
  3. Assessment day
  4. Offer & place

1. Register interest

Two minutes online. You'll get the prospectus and the current fee schedule immediately.

2. Visit the school

An Open Morning or a private tour on a normal working day — we recommend the latter.

3. Assessment day

Age-appropriate papers plus a conversation. No tutoring required, and we mean it.

4. Offer & place

Offers go out in February. Bursary decisions arrive at the same time, never later.

What are the fees, and are bursaries available?
Fees for 2027–28 run from £4,850 per term in Prep to £6,420 in Sixth Form, including lunches and most trips. Around one pupil in six holds a means-tested bursary, up to 100% of fees.
Do you require tutoring before assessment?
No — and heavily tutored candidates are usually obvious to us. The papers are designed to show how a child thinks, not what they have been drilled on.
Can we join mid-year?
Occasionally, where a place comes free. Register your interest and we will contact you if one opens in your child's year group.
What support is there for SEN?
We have four full-time specialists and support around 90 pupils with identified needs. Talk to us early so we can be honest about whether we're the right setting.
