Accordion

Rebuilt on native <details>/<summary>. The name attribute gives you exclusive groups — no Collapse JS at all.

Zero JavaScript
The browser handles show/hide. Nothing to initialise, and it works before your bundle loads.
Exclusive by name
Sharing a name makes opening one close the others — this replaces data-bs-parent.
Searchable and linkable
Browser find-in-page can open a closed <details>, which a div-based accordion never could.

Dialog was Modal

Built on the native <dialog> element — the browser supplies the backdrop, focus trap and inert top layer, so .modal-dialog and .modal-content are gone.

Drawer was Offcanvas

Also a native <dialog>. Four edges.

Menu was Dropdown

No .dropdown wrapper and no .dropdown-toggle — the toggle and the .menu are siblings. Positioned by Floating UI.
