FAQ

Questions we get a lot

Do we need a credit card to start?
No. The 14-day trial starts on signup with no card. If you don't upgrade, the workspace drops to the free tier — nothing is deleted.
Can we migrate from Jira or Linear?
Yes. The importer maps issues, labels, milestones, and comment history, and shows you a full diff before anything is written. Most migrations under 5,000 issues finish in about ten minutes.
How does per-seat billing handle contractors?
Guest seats are free for up to 5 people per project and are read-plus-comment only. Convert a guest to a full seat any time; billing prorates to the day.
Where is our data stored?
US and EU regions are available on every plan; Business adds Australia and Canada. Data never leaves the region you pick, including backups.
What happens if we cancel?
You keep read access indefinitely and can export everything to JSON or Markdown at any point — no support ticket required.
