Product

Three primitives, one balance you can reconcile

Most stacks make you keep your own shadow ledger to answer "where is the money right now". This one answers it.

Payments

Cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay, direct debit and open banking through one intent object. 3-D Secure only when the risk engine asks for it.

Auth rate 94.1% across all merchants, last 30 days.

Payouts

Pay out to sellers, contractors or your own accounts on a schedule you set. Failed payouts come back with the bank's actual reason, not a generic code.

Faster Payments in seconds, SEPA and SWIFT where it has to be.

Ledger API

Real double-entry accounts with immutable postings. Every movement has two sides and the whole thing balances or the write is rejected.

Idempotency keys on every endpoint, held for 24 hours.

Developers

One request to take a payment

No SDK required and no six-step handshake. Post an intent, get a client secret, confirm it. The ledger postings are created for you and appear in the same response.

  • Idempotent by default — retries cannot double-charge
  • Webhooks signed with a rotating secret, replayable for 30 days
  • Test and live keys differ by one character, so mistakes are loud
