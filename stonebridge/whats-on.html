Stonebridge · What's on

The whole week,
as it actually runs

Everything below happens every week without needing to be announced. Dated one-offs are further down, and anything that has already happened says so.

Sunday

  • 10.30am

    Sunday Morning

    Our main gathering. Songs, a talk, coffee afterwards that goes on longer than the service. Kids' groups leave after fifteen minutes.

    Main church

  • 6.30pm

    Sunday Evening

    Quieter and mostly sung, with communion every week. Around forty of us, and nobody minds which seat you take.

    Side chapel

Tuesday

  • 10.00am

    Community Pantry

    Food, toiletries and a cup of tea. No referral needed and no questions asked.

    Church hall

  • 1.30pm

    Bereavement Café

    For anyone who has lost someone, however long ago. Tea, cake, and no pressure to speak.

    Meeting room

Wednesday

  • 10.00am

    Little Steps

    Toddler group — toys out, songs at eleven, toast for everyone at quarter past.

    Church hall

  • 6.00pm

    The Wednesday Table

    A free hot meal for anyone who wants one, then twenty minutes of something worth talking about. No sign-up, no collection.

    Church hall

Thursday

  • 8.00am

    Morning Prayer

    Half an hour, said not sung, done by half eight so you can still get to work.

    Side chapel

  • 7.30pm

    Choir Practice

    We rehearse for Sunday and for the carol service. You do not need to read music.

    Main church

Friday

  • 7.00pm

    Youth (school years 7–13)

    Games, food, and a short discussion. Ends at nine, and we walk everyone to the bus stop.

    Church hall

Saturday

  • 9.30am

    Churchyard Working Party

    Tools provided, bacon sandwiches at eleven. Come for an hour or the whole morning.

    Churchyard

Coming up

One-offs, festivals and the fête

These carry real dates, so anything in the past greys itself out rather than sitting here pretending to be news.

  • 27 Sep 2026

    Harvest Festival Passed

    10.30am · Main church

    Bring tinned goods if you can. Everything collected goes to the pantry that week.

  • 24 Dec 2026

    Crib Service Passed

    4.00pm · Main church

    Thirty minutes, aimed squarely at under-sevens. Costumes strongly encouraged.

  • 26 Mar 2027

    Good Friday: The Cross in Music Passed

    7.30pm · Main church

    Our choir and a string quartet. Free, no ticket needed, doors from seven.

  • 28 Mar 2027

    Easter Sunrise on Bridge Hill Passed

    6.15am · Bridge Hill

    Twenty minutes at first light, then breakfast back at the hall. Wear boots.

  • 16 May 2027

    Parish Lunch Passed

    12.30pm · Church hall

    Roast, pudding, and the annual meeting nobody can escape. £6, under-12s free.

  • 26 Jun 2027

    Summer Fair Passed

    2.00pm · Churchyard

    Stalls, cakes, a very competitive tombola. All proceeds to the pantry.

Room hire

The building is for the town, not just for Sundays

Rehearsals, AGMs, birthday parties, polling. Community groups pay half, and nobody has ever been charged for a wake.

A session is a morning, afternoon or evening. Rates held since 2025.
Space Capacity Rate Notes
Main church 180 seated £45 / session Piano, sound system, induction loop. No catering.
Church hall 90 seated, 120 standing £30 / session Kitchen, tables and chairs, level access from Market Street.
Meeting room 16 around a table £15 / session Screen, kettle, quiet. Upstairs — no lift.
Churchyard Outdoor By arrangement For fairs and fêtes. Talk to the office first.
