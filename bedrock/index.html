Process

From enquiry
to handover

Five stages, and we will tell you at stage two if we think the programme in the tender documents is undeliverable.

  1. Enquiry & site visit
  2. Buildability review
  3. Tender submission
  4. Mobilisation
  5. Handover & validation

Buildability review comes before price

We mark up the drawings and come back with what we would change. Roughly a third of our tenders include a compliant bid and an alternative.

One team, start to finish

The estimator who priced it attends the pre-start. The agent who ran the groundworks signs off the validation report.

Weekly cost and programme

Applications go in on the same day each month and progress photos are shared on Fridays whether or not anyone asks.
